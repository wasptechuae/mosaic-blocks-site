# Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}). Please include your phone model and its Android or iOS version. English or Arabic are both welcome. تفضّل بالكتابة بالعربية أو الإنجليزية.

## FAQ

<details markdown="1"><summary>Where is my progress saved?</summary>

On your phone only. There are no accounts and no game server, so progress does not move to a new phone and is lost if you uninstall the game.

</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Why are there ads, and how do I change my choice?</summary>

Ads keep Mosaic Blocks free. Some you choose to watch for a free tool, to keep going, or to double a Daily Reward; others appear between some games. Where the law requires it, a Google consent form asks about ads when you first open the game. Change your answer any time in the game under **Settings, Privacy options** (the row shows only where it applies). See the [privacy policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).

</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Does the game need a connection?</summary>

No. It plays offline; ads simply will not appear.

</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Are there in-app purchases?</summary>

No. Coins are earned by playing.

</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>How do I switch to Arabic?</summary>

Open **Settings** in the game and choose العربية under Language. The whole game flips to right-to-left.

</details>
